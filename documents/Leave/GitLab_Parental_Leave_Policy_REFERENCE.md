## Parental Leave Policy — GitLab Inc.

**Source:** GitLab Inc., People Group Handbook — Leave Types
**URL:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/time-off-and-absence/leave-types/
**License:** Publicly published company handbook — reference use only, not public domain

### Key Policy Points
- GitLab provides 16 weeks of paid Parental Leave to any active team member, regardless of gender, who becomes a parent through childbirth or adoption.
- The benefit is available starting a team member's first day of employment, provided the child is born or adopted while they are employed at GitLab.
- The full 16-week entitlement must be used before the child's first birthday, or the first anniversary of the adoption.
- Parental leave may be taken all at once or split into as many as three separate segments.
- Local or state statutory parental leave benefits may provide additional entitlements on top of the company policy, depending on the team member's country or U.S. state.

### Planning and Scheduling
- GitLab calculates leave dates using a 1-week-equals-7-days formula, so a return on a Monday means the leave officially ends the preceding Sunday.
- Team members are encouraged to begin their leave when the child arrives, but can coordinate a different start date with the Absence Management team.
- If additional time is needed after parental leave ends, a team member may use the standard PTO policy with manager coordination.
