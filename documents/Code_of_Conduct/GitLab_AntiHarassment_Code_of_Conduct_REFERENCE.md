## Anti-Harassment Policy and Ethics Reporting — GitLab Inc.

**Source:** GitLab Inc., People Group Handbook — Anti-Harassment Policy
**URL:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/anti-harassment/
**License:** Publicly published company handbook — reference use only, not public domain

### Key Policy Points
- GitLab maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment of any kind, applying equally to all team members and contractors regardless of location.
- The policy covers not just internal team interactions but also a team member's conduct toward customers, vendors, and community members.
- Every team member shares responsibility for preventing and stopping harassment when they witness it, not just HR or management.
- Local labor and state laws, where applicable, are followed in addition to this company-wide policy when handling and investigating incidents.

### Reporting Channels
- Suspected harassment can be reported directly to the Chief People Officer, the Team Member Relations group, or a People Business Partner for investigation.
- Team members can also submit a Harassment Complaint Form directly to the Team Member Relations Specialists.
- Broader ethics or compliance violations can be reported through EthicsPoint, a hotline and online platform available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, which accepts anonymous or named reports; the U.S. toll-free hotline number is 1-833-756-0853.
- All team members must complete compliance training — covering anti-harassment, security, data privacy, and insider trading — within 30 days of starting at the company.
