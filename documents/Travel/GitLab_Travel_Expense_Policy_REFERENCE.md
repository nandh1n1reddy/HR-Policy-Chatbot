## Global Travel and Expense Policy — GitLab Inc.

**Source:** GitLab Inc., Finance Handbook — Expenses
**URL:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/finance/expenses/
**License:** Publicly published company handbook — reference use only, not public domain

### Key Policy Points
- Team members must get their manager's approval before booking any business-critical trip, and should inform their manager of travel plans in advance so the trip fits the departmental budget.
- Only expenses that are "ordinary and necessary" for business purposes are reimbursable; virtual meetings should be considered before booking travel.
- All expenses must be submitted by the end of the fiscal quarter in which they were incurred. GitLab's fiscal quarters end January 31, April 30, July 31, and October 31.
- For meal expenses while traveling, team members are reimbursed at a standard per diem rate (based on U.S. GSA rates) and do not need to keep receipts for per-diem meal claims.
- The company does not reimburse travel or immigration costs for a team member's family members or significant other, including for company events.

### Flight and Lodging Upgrades
- On flights longer than 8 hours, a team member taller than 1.95m (6'5") can be reimbursed up to $300 for a Business Class upgrade.
- On flights longer than 8 hours, any team member can be reimbursed up to $100 for an Economy Plus upgrade, regardless of height.
- Lodging outside the company's standard rate guidelines requires pre-approval from an authorized approver before booking.
