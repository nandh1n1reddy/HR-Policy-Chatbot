## All-Remote and Asynchronous Work Policy — GitLab Inc.

**Source:** GitLab Inc., Company Culture Handbook — All-Remote
**URL:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/company/culture/all-remote/
**License:** Publicly published company handbook — reference use only, not public domain

### Key Policy Points
- GitLab operates as a fully remote company with no central office; team members work from home offices across dozens of countries and time zones.
- There are no fixed core working hours. Team members choose when they work based on their own energy levels, preferences, and time zone.
- Performance is measured by results and completed work, not hours logged or real-time availability.
- Work defaults to asynchronous collaboration — written documentation and issue threads are preferred over live meetings, and meetings are expected to have a clear agenda and be optional where possible.
- The company handbook itself serves as the single source of truth for policies and processes, so team members are encouraged to check the handbook before asking a colleague directly.

### In-Person Gathering Cadence
- Leadership (the "e-group") meets in person for a structured offsite roughly once per quarter.
- The wider team gathers in person for a shared company-wide event approximately every 9 to 12 months.
- Team members with 5 years of tenure become eligible for a 25-business-day sabbatical.
