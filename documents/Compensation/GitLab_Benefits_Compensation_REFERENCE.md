## Retirement Match and Growth & Development Benefits — GitLab Inc.

**Source:** GitLab Inc., Total Rewards Handbook — General and Entity Benefits
**URL:** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/benefits/general-and-entity-benefits/
**License:** Publicly published company handbook — reference use only, not public domain

### Key Policy Points
- GitLab matches 50% of a team member's retirement plan contributions on the first 6% of their base salary, up to a yearly company match cap of $1,500.
- Company matching contributions vest evenly over 4 years of service.
- Team members can be reimbursed up to $10,000 USD per calendar year for continuing education and professional growth, subject to tenure, performance, business need, and available budget.
- A course or program counts toward the calendar year in which it is paid for or reimbursed; courses finishing in December get a grace period until end of January to file the prior year's claim.
